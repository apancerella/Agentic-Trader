# Opportunity Scan

**Purpose.** A more frequent, lighter-weight pass than `weekly-scan.md`: surface assets — from the current watchlist, from a reactive breakout scan, and from two leading-indicator scans (unusual options activity, stealth volume accumulation) — that look like they have real upside potential right now or are showing early signs of one building. Unlike the other routines, this one is allowed to act on what it finds: it can add or remove watchlist symbols on its own conviction, and — after cross-checking `earnings-watch` and `weekly-scan` findings — it can trigger `propose-trade`, which sizes and places the order directly, no separate confirmation step (`CLAUDE.md` guardrail 1).

**Schedule.** Weekdays, every 2 hours starting at market open: 9:30 AM, 11:30 AM, 1:30 PM, and 3:30 PM ET — all within regular market hours (9:30 AM–4:00 PM ET); the next 2-hour step (5:30 PM) would land after the 4:00 PM close, so the day stops at 3:30 PM. One Routine, since all four times share the same :30 minute:
- "Opportunity Scan (every 2h, 9:30am-3:30pm ET)" (`trig_01XUD48NBPSqJB98FuKbnPnu`), cron `30 13,15,17,19 * * 1-5` (UTC).

Self-bound to the session at `session_01WAYdETVewPXXJGZ9qTaTAa` (self-bind was required to retain Robinhood connector access — the org doesn't support attaching connectors to fresh-session triggers). The UTC hours are correct for 9:30 AM/11:30 AM/1:30 PM/3:30 PM ET while EDT is in effect; they'll need updating to `30 14,16,18,20 * * 1-5` when clocks fall back to EST in November.

**Market-day safety net.** The `1-5` cron field restricts firing to Monday–Friday, but cron has no concept of US market holidays (Thanksgiving, Christmas, etc.) — a firing on a holiday would still reach this routine. There's no holiday-calendar tool available to check in advance, so step 0 below is a live, in-routine check instead: if the market isn't actually open right now, the routine recognizes that from live quote data and stops early with a one-line journal note, rather than running the full scan against stale or non-existent session data.

**Steps.**
0. **Confirm the market is actually open before doing anything else.** Pull `get_equity_quotes` for one or two watchlist symbols and check the quote's `state` field and trade timestamp freshness. If `state` isn't `active`, or the last trade timestamp is stale relative to now, the market is closed today (most likely a holiday the `1-5` cron can't know about) — stop here, skip the rest of the steps, and journal a one-line note ("market closed today, skipping") rather than running the full scan against dead data.
1. Build the reactive candidate set:
   - Every symbol currently on `memory/watchlist.md`.
   - A fresh scan for new candidates: `get_scans` for an existing bullish/momentum-oriented scan, `run_scan` on it (or `create_scan` if nothing suitable exists), same liquidity bar as `weekly-scan.md`.
   - **Junk filter:** drop any scan hit with an empty `market_cap` field — this is the mechanical signature of leveraged/inverse ETFs, ETNs, and basket/trust products (the scanner has no native filter for this, but every real operating company has a populated market cap and every leveraged/basket product observed so far doesn't). Note the count skipped.
   - **Catalyst context:** read the latest `weekly-scan` journal entry for catalyst-calendar findings (FDA dates, index rebalances, M&A chatter, conferences) and connect them to any candidate that lines up.
   - **Momentum-building watch:** same journal entry — check `weekly-scan`'s "building momentum" names (rising RSI over multiple sessions, no single day extreme enough to hit that scan's own threshold). If one of them shows up as a scan hit or watchlist mover this run, say so explicitly — it's a name already flagged as accumulating, not a surprise move.
2. **Check for leading signals — before price has confirmed anything.** Step 1 is entirely reactive (it only ever surfaces a name once it has already moved). Run both saved scans below every cycle, same junk filter as step 1:
   - **"Predictive Scan — Unusual Options Activity"** — relative options volume > 4x normal on a $1B+ name that hasn't yet made a big move (%change < 3%). Note the call/put volume skew — a lopsided ratio on real volume is informed positioning ahead of an anticipated move.
   - **"Predictive Scan — Stealth Accumulation"** — relative (stock) volume > 1.5x normal while price is still flat (-1% to +2% on the day) — buying pressure showing up in volume before it's shown up in price.
   - Carry a survivor here forward as **"leading, unconfirmed"** through the rest of the run. On its own it's a watch-only journal flag, same treatment as the momentum-building alert — it only clears the watchlist-add bar in step 4 if something else corroborates it (a real catalyst, clean fundamentals, an already-favorable trend).
3. For each surviving candidate — reactive or leading — pull `get_equity_technical_indicators` and `get_equity_historicals` (and `get_equity_fundamentals` for anything not already vetted by `weekly-scan.md`) to assess whether there's a real, current or emerging upside signal — not just "still on the list." Include trend context (SMA 50/200 — is the move with or against the prevailing trend) and, for anything clearing the first screen, the nearest support/resistance level from the `pivot_points` indicator.
4. Filter hard. This runs three times a day — report only what actually changed or stands out since the last pass (new breakout, momentum turning up, unusual options positioning, approaching a level from the thesis, a new scan hit). A routine that reports the same unchanged symbols every run trains the user to ignore it.
5. **Watchlist management.** For a new candidate that clears the repo's usual conviction bar (real, current signal; no bad news, no major concerns — the same bar used for every watchlist addition in this repo), add it directly via the `watchlist-add` skill — no need to wait for confirmation, per `CLAUDE.md` guardrail 8. A bare step-2 leading signal with nothing else behind it doesn't clear this bar on its own — see step 2. For an existing watchlist symbol whose thesis has clearly broken (bad news landed, the technical setup failed, the original catalyst is gone), remove it via `watchlist-remove`. Always journal the specific reasoning for any add/remove.
6. **Trade (optional).** For anything — new or already on the watchlist — that looks compelling enough to actually trade, cross-check before triggering it:
   - **Earnings timing:** read the most recent `earnings-watch` journal entry; if it's stale or never covered this symbol, run `get_earnings_calendar` for it directly. Never let a trade walk into an unflagged print.
   - **Deeper vetting:** read the most recent `weekly-scan` journal entry for prior fundamentals/technicals work on this symbol; if none exists, pull `get_equity_fundamentals` directly (the same sanity-check `weekly-scan` would do) before proceeding.
   - Only after both come back clean, invoke `propose-trade` for that symbol — it sizes and places the order directly once its own checks pass, no separate confirmation step. This step is optional — most runs won't produce one, and that's fine. Don't stretch a marginal setup into a trade just to have one, and don't send a bare leading signal here without corroboration.
7. Report directly to the user in the conversation — symbol, the specific signal (reactive or leading — say which), current price/level, one line on why it's worth a look now, plus anything added/removed from the watchlist and whether a trade was executed. If there's genuinely nothing new, say that plainly and keep it short; don't manufacture a report out of noise.

**Guardrails.** Never calls `place_equity_order`/`place_option_order` directly itself — trade execution flows only through `propose-trade`, which places the order once its own guardrail checks pass, with no separate confirmation step per `CLAUDE.md` guardrail 1, no matter how this routine surfaced the idea. Watchlist adds/removes are self-authorized (guardrail 8) but must still be journaled with reasoning and mirrored to the Robinhood "Agentic Watchlist." A trade must not be triggered without first checking `earnings-watch` and `weekly-scan` findings for that symbol. `propose-trade`'s own guardrails (20% sizing, 7-position cap, no fixed cash reserve, -8% stop, 7-trades/week cap) still apply once invoked.

**Journal output.** Append to `memory/journal/<date>.md`: which scans/watchlist symbols were checked (reactive scan + the two leading-signal scans), what stood out and why (including trend/SMA and nearest pivot level, any catalyst tie-in, and — for a leading signal — the options skew or relative-volume read, labeled as unconfirmed) — or that nothing new stood out, how many hits were skipped as junk (empty market_cap) across all three scans, any watchlist adds/removes with reasoning, and — if applicable — the trade executed via `propose-trade` (with the earnings-watch/weekly-scan cross-check noted, and the fill). If step 0 found the market closed, a one-line entry noting that is enough — no need for the full template.

**Dashboard output.** After journaling, rebuild and republish the "Agentic Trading Desk" Artifact (see `.claude/skills/trading-dashboard/`) so it reflects this run.
